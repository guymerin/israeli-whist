Privacy Policy

Israeli Whist · Effective July 29, 2026

Short version: Israeli Whist collects no personal data, has no analytics or tracking, contains no ads, and makes no network requests beyond loading the app itself. Everything stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, request, transmit, or store any personal information about you. There are no accounts, logins, or sign‑ups.

Data stored on your device

To let you resume play, the app saves a small amount of game state locally on your device using standard on‑device storage: the display name you optionally enter, your scores, and your game history. This data never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is removed if you delete the app.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

The app contains no analytics, no third‑party SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking technologies. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across apps or websites.

Third parties

We do not share, sell, or disclose any information to third parties, because we do not collect any.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
